First public release. Import kubeconfigs (file or paste), manage multiple
clusters and contexts, browse workloads, pods, services and custom
resources with official Kubernetes icons, status pills and a fleet health
bar; pod logs with follow, save and session-persistent view toggles;
pull-to-refresh; safe in-app editing behind a confirmation gate; dynamic
Material You theming with a Kubernetes-blue fallback. Keystore-encrypted
credential storage, zero telemetry, no camera permission.
